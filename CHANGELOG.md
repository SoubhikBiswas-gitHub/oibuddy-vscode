# Changes

## 0.3.0 preview

- Meet your buddy in more places: beside the line numbers, in the status bar and on a wide shelf next to the Terminal. Turn each one on or off in Settings.
- Find your focus sound: original lofi tracks and calm rain, fireplace, café, night and wave ambience. Sounds loop, shuffle without repeats, get quieter during visits and can follow your focus sessions, even when the view is hidden.
- Watch your buddy walk in on painted legs when a reminder arrives. On the shelf, it strolls, sometimes runs, waves and makes a little hop. Choose Later for a gentle sigh. Shelf motion pauses while you type, while a reminder waits, during visits and when motion is reduced.
- Hear soft cues for guided breaks, a breath for each inhale when voice is off, and a water pour when you log a glass. Sound effects now play at an even volume.
- Read clearer, kinder wording in English, Hindi and Bengali, with consistent names across screens.
- Download less: the buddies' paintings and voices take less than half the space, with no visible or audible change.

## 0.2.0 preview: the Windowsill

- Redesign every screen as "the Windowsill": the buddy lives on a shelf drawn over your own VS Code theme, with bundled Anek and Kalam fonts for English, Hindi and Bengali, keycap buttons and the day shown as one timeline.
- Give the buddy a living home in the sidebar: it reads while you type, nods when you save, naps when you are away, welcomes you back, and peeks in politely when a reminder is waiting. A jar fills with today's water and a sprout grows with your streak.
- Let people rename their buddy; names appear everywhere in text, notifications and the status bar.
- Wait for a natural pause before a reminder walks in, count only real time away from the keyboard as a break, and offer a short break when a focus session ends.
- Add timed pauses (30 minutes, 1 hour, until the next working day), a "+1 glass" water log, quick actions, and a live status bar that shows what is coming next.
- Add "Do it with me" guided breaks, choices for how long Later lasts, and fresher rotating companion lines.
- Add a three-step first-run setup, settings that save on their own, millilitres or ounces, weekly progress with kind streaks, and suggestions when a reminder keeps getting skipped.
- Introduce the Peek icon for the Activity Bar, status bar and Marketplace, plus new stretch poses and calm sound effects.

## 0.1.1 preview

- Add a visible Activity Bar entry and dashboard buttons in the compact companion view.
- Preserve settings drafts and merge independent preference changes safely across windows.
- Reject stale reminder edits; apply new quiet hours to active scheduled visits.
- Count Done, Later or Skip tapped up to 15 minutes after a visit times out, once, unless a newer visit for that reminder has started.
- After quiet hours, sleep or time away, show one overdue reminder and restart the others instead of a burst of back-to-back visits.
- Bring a missed visit back after the snooze time once, then return to its normal interval.
- Stop recording pause, focus, settings changes and reminder edits as snoozes or skips in history.
- Show when focus ends and when Later will return, a countdown on each visit, and focus or outside-hours state in the status bar.
- Translate error messages into Hindi and Bengali and show all times in 12-hour format.
- Connect arrival, action, waiting, acknowledgement and departure sequences with one-shot playback and distinct responses.
- Add loading deadlines, bounded playback timers, disposal cleanup and live system reduced-motion support.
- Improve dark-theme text and progress visibility. Character motion and native listening approval remain pending.

## 0.1.0 preview

- Add seven selectable companions, a dashboard and a compact companion panel.
- Add water, stretch, eye-rest, walking and custom interval reminders.
- Add English, Hindi and Bengali interface selection, with English as the default.
- Add working days, overnight hours, snooze, pause, focus sessions and local progress.
- Add minute-precise hours, configurable focus duration and audio volume, per-reminder snooze, and eight editable habit templates.
- Coordinate reminders across windows and choose one visible presentation surface per window.
- Preserve saved data during recovery, serialize writes, and reject unsupported newer formats.
- Repair stale animation draws, visibility resume, repeated entrances and interrupted playback.

Character motion, recording approval, sound effects and platform verification remain release gates. This preview is not the final public release.
